Each of us goes to The Club for our own reasons. Maybe it is to meet an old friend, find a new friend, look for someone special, or just to relax after a hard day of work or school (in some cases, actually relaxing DURING work or school). The point is, we are each here for a reason. Personally, I (and you know me as "Gentleman") have met some tremendous friends through the club, and whatever else happens, I will be eternally grateful for the opportunity to meet Donna, Huggabear, Danae, Squeeky and others. However, from the start, I have felt that The Club lacked one thing; a central place for us to discuss specific topics of interest. For the sports fan, would it not be nice to have a specific room at a specific time devoted to the NBA and NHL playoffs? Or, how about a session on job interviewing skills? Or, any other topic that generates some interest.

I have been put in charge of developing the calendar of events portion of the club. My vision, as I see it, is to develop an area of the club to hold these discussions on a regular basis. Some topics, like job interviewing skills, I would moderate. Others, like the NHL talk, I would seek club members as experts. The first step in planning this area is to get your feedback and ideas. What topics would you like to see covered? To suggest ideas, please catch me when I am in the club or e-mail me at gentleman@clubgabbay.com. I look forward to working with your ideas to make this area a success and a learning experience for all!
