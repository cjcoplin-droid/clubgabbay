Journey to Destiny

by Michael Gabbay

Star date 9/17/97
Studio Log entry 11
3rd and final day at SOUNDCITY, Van Nuys, Ca. 12:00 p.m. call
Attendees: Myself, Walter, Scott, Earl and Kelly and Bill

Guys, I just got the pics from the studio...Walter and Bill snapped shots and I took a pic of 'Rewind' the studio cat!!! Check the shots out, a couple of me...a couple of Scott...a great one of Walter....a group shot and of coarse...one of Earl and Kelly!!!

Man oh man!!! I learned and did so much yesterday wENS, 9/17!!! The date had a 12:00 call, I showed up early, I'll be honest I knew we were getting the work done and was very happy with the tracks...but I wasn't feeling in sync somehow SOMETHING WAS MISSING ...cause I wanted more...longer days...more of everything!!! but remember my resolve to just 'go with the flow'...Tuesday night, granted at the end of the day I was tired but we were out of the studio at 7:00 p.m. I was home early...Bev and I went to my fav chinese restaurant on the way home, so we were home at about 8:30 or so...I have an idea that the next days work could really go smoothly and we might be out of the studio in another 7 hr day...now let me note that we locked out our room for 3 days at 700.00 a day without getting any grief from the studio we could be there for approx. 12 hours a day (but there is a true human element to how much a persons ears can really stand before there hearing is somewhat impaired and making decisions they might not normally make...you gotta know when to quit...and Walter does) so far we were only doing 7 hour days...that would bring the studio costs to a 100 bucks an hour...that adds up pretty quickly, don't you think???...well I'm thinking hey maybe if it's cool with everyone I can get them to do "Cyerano's heart" and "How do I get there from here" so while I was waiting for everyone to show up I sat at the piano and started doing a chart of the 2 tunes, by the time everyone walked in I had completed the charts...I threw the idea out there...but everyone really wanted to see how the day went...so I put it out there and left it at that...

O.K. here's a run down on the tunes...1st song of the day, "I do believe" this song tracked fairly smoothly because the first verse is guitar alone, the band only had to deal with the 2nd verse to the end of the tune...know bumps in the rd. this tune went down without a struggle!!! Song number 2, "In another mans house" this song is truely a gem, we knew it and we wanted to capture it, we worked it and worked it...Scotts piano on this in nothing less than stunning, but it is challenging for him to play and sing this at the same time and frankly the band needs the ques from the vocal, Scott decides to play the tune in block chords and preform his part after the track is cut...he does just that, I stood over his shoulder while he preformed his part...and man I was watching his hands...and hearing the sound in my blairing headphones and trust me when I tell you that, that Stienway was thundering alla Scott Doyle, I WAS PROUD TO BE HIS PARTNER...TO MYSELF, I GAVE THANKS...Walter buys the preformance and we are done...this song took approx an hour and half maybe to get a good take, the longest song so far...but well worth it!!! Next tune..."Find your way home" we play it a couple of times looking for the tempo, find something that feels great, Kelly slips on a pair of shades and lets the click track take him away...and take him away it does...understand it is harder to play slow than it is to play fast...you need to be very exact with the quarter notes!!! Kelly gets up after his take and he is 120% his preformance is flawless...he listens back in an amazement at his own preformance (he wasn't being concieted as much as he was confident and frankly he was right...his preformance was right there) He's giving off a vibe that he's not gonna do another take and Walter isn't objecting, on the play back Scott had a thing or 2 to fix and Earl had 2 notes to redo...Scott takes care of his performance and so does Earl...a quick word about Walter, the guy is awesome, his ears for time and preformance are just unreal watching him work is viewing his art...I will get into stuff about Walter in a bit...I need to sing his praise...read on...Tune 4, "Put your hand on the radio" remember I've told you give us something uptempo and we are gonna eat it up...boom!!!! Kelly counts this one in and we are gone!!! 2 three takes and it's done...rock, rock, rock around the clock, it just felt great!!! O.K. break time, hey know talk of food so far HOW NICE!!! We all seem to really be dailed in, I had noticed for myself that I was intight with click on this day more than on the 2 previous days I was keenly tuned into the whole trip I felt close to my guitar, I felt more in the moment than I think I have ever been...this was the feeling I wanted to have on day one...Well it's break time and remember I threw the thought of doing these tunes out...Scott was a doll he was willing to take a look so he and I sit in the studio, I have my guitar on and Scott is listening at the paino and I get through "Cyerano's" and I get the, "VERY COOL" high sign from Scott and Walter joins us right about then, He had heard "Cyerano's" and I think he liked it, I played "How do I get there from here" understand, I can barely sing and singing and playing is an art unto itself, the tune has a high chorus and I couldn't really show in it's proper light there both were alittle standoff-ish...but I ask for thier trust because I've heard Bevy sing this song and I am certain that the song has the magic, they give me there trust and agree to cut the tunes!!! This really means alot cause it's a testimony to the tunes themselves, right around now Walter opens a dicussion that Scott excuses himself from, he went to get something to eat, I'll get into my chat with Walter when I discuss Walter more in detail...Meanwhile we had 2 tunes left to cut and on one of the tunes, Walters gotta make a change in the drum sounds...after a good break, Scott walks back in the room just as Walter and I were finishing that chat...know less than 20-25 mins!!! We call the reggae tune "What's in it for me" Man we grooved it...it was unreal the intensity of the groove is so confident...dance!!! dance!!! dance!!! WE WERE ALL PLAESED!!!

O.K. with Walter and Scotts support Kelly and Earl agree to cut the tunes (they are being compensated for there time) We start running "Cyerano's" Walter hears it almost like PROCUL HAIREM'S, "WHITER SHADE OF PALE" Scott plays the hammond organ, the magic sets into the room...and there we are well into our 8th hour...know sign of fatigue and we are all right there...the magic of the tune takes over...a couple of things get worked on...the sections are worked out and we print a take...OH YEAH WALTER DECIDED WE SHOULD HAVE DINNER BROUGHT IN FROM THAT BBQ PLACE...I FORK OVER THE CREDIT CARD...70.00 BUCKS WORTH!!! We listen to the tune and break for dinner!!! Well I sit down and I'm getting that, I'm getting what I came to the studio for...creating with the guys as opposed to rehearsing the tunes with them is a whole different smoke, it was so empowering and phofilling, I mean there is something to be said for the spontanious combustion of doing a tune on the spot, but on the other hand it does take alot more studio time and frankly in the case of some of these songs, some of the subtelties of the tune gets over looked if the players are learning them and then recording thier first impression, Scott and I both agree in this case, it was better for us to rehearse this then to take a chance just going in, not that Earl and Kelly couldn't pull it off...these are the guys you want on your team!!! I have some ribs, exchange some thoughts with Walter and I sit back and let the dinner break go on for as long as the guys want to break...hey thats really something for me, but these guys know what needs to be done...know need to rush anything...the work we came for is in the can except for the Casino's cover...The dinner break does finally come to an end and we are making 'Cyerano' himself take notice!!! We cut the tune in about a half hour and listened to the play back and these guys are saying "BEST SONG SO FAR!!!" WHOA!!! 'Cyerano's' might just make the swim upstream and make it too the cd, I really had know preconcieved notion of springing the tunes on the guys...it just happened and maybe it happened for a reason...I felt very touched...there are some feelings I have yet to share with you and I think today might be the day to get into that...

O.K., "How do I get there from here" the tune is a bit more complex than I write, I believe in it all the way to bank...but it was charted with 3 measures of 2/4 in different places and it was gonna take some work to cut...without words both Kelly and Earl get up and are just about leave the room...it was amazing as muscians they were really rejecting the tune...what was amazing about this is, that if a song is too difficult to preform, it's almost like a certain death certificate (i.e. some Bacharach/David tunes) cause I mean who wants to play a song thats tuff to learn or easy to feel...well leave it to Walter to jump right out of the controll room and grab a lead sheet and look at the damage...O.K. he instructs me to play the chorus...I do, and whe says...'cut this'...DONE!!! I play alittle more he says cut that!!! DONE!!! and on the last measure that is funny...he see's the value and says leave that one in...Kelly and Earl walk back in and Walter instructs them on the changes and within know time..."How do I get there" is a done deal...we cut 2 takes one a medium tempo and another a slower take, we didn't listen back before we moved on, but we were all positive that the last taked grooved real well...I know what I heard in the phones and I was blissed at how we all were right in the moment together...without so much as looking at each other, we all were in the same place at the same time but we were focusing on our own preformances and as a result we met right at the tune and played it wonderfully...I felt 10 feet tall!!!

O.k. one last tune, the time now is about 9:30 or so...Walter has to make a few changes to the drums because we are gonna cut this song as a jazzy lounge type tune, adjustments made...we work the tune over and I can see Scott is running on empty, we cut it in about 3 or 4 passes...Walter pops out of the controll room and clearly states he doesn't think this tune is gonna make the final cut, he thinks we'd have better luck with it playing a wedding... I had a reason for choosing a cover tune, I was thinking of marketing, it's a wondeful song, it is a feel we don't cover and the tune is a proven winner, sometimes in braking an artist a good cover can get attention, I mean you have to look at the angels...and the bottomline is I thopught it fit in...I'll reserve judgement until I myself hear the tune!!!

And so we come to the end of this leg of the journey, I am a better person for the expierence and very proud to be the writer, and to get a chance to play on these tunes with players of this caliber, I feel very lucky indeed but I do have some feeling I'll try to write about...read on...

Scott and I need to discuss business so he and I agree to speak the next day, and WALTER AND I HAVE TO TALK ABOUT WHAT'S NEXT...I know the tunes are go into to editing, but I don't know when work will resume...And there you have it...all the news that fit to print...I will now try to give my thoughts on each guys and what it's like working shoulder to shoulder with them.

Walter...as you know he is producing, what does a producer do??? well I'll tell you what this one does and you'll have to trust me...this one is a PRO!!! So far to date, Walter gracefully got himself into this project, developed a relationship with Scott and I, he viewed my first choice of players, when we were unable to strike a deal, he delivers the right guys at the right price!!! Gets meetings, charts, and tapes made and makes sure that Kelly and Earl come prepared. In the studio...get this..NOT ONE TECHNICAL ERROR...HE IS IN COMPLETE COMMAND OF THE CONTROLL ROOM. His ability to listen to a rhythm section and communicate with the guys is impressive...he get's right to the point in a direct know b.s. manner but he never pisses anyone off...His abilty to listen to music on tape and hear flaws in the preformances is exacting!!! this guy is dead on it's scary especially when you don't hear it and you are sitting right next to him!!! Next...remember that chat Walter and I had, well here goes...it was about paying Earl and Kelly, this opens up alot of feeling I want to discuss with you but I'll try to talk around the undercurrent of this chat and tell you what went on...Walter says...I should give the guys something for playing these 2 tunes and I'm thinking these guys would do it for free and you know what, if Walter asked they'd do it...but Walter knows the value of taking care of the memebers of his team...man this guy was very persistant on these guys getting thier money and I was trying to explain my point...and for arguments sake lets even say my points were valid, Walter wasn't backing down, without going into the details...the bottom line is Walter got his men an agreeable amount of money and there was a good feeling about it...had he not gone the distance,"Cyerano's" might not of had the magic that it has...his insitance was well worth the result!!! Finally when it came to the arrangement of, "How do I get there" he didn't waste a sec. making sure the music was right, he helped improve the quality of the song and saved time and the vibe in the room at that!!!

THIS IS ONLY A SMALL PART OF THE JOB OF A PRODUCER...WE STILL HAVE A LONG WAY TO GO...AND THE LIST OF THINGS WALTER HAS YET TO DO IS A LONG ONE...(WHEN YOU GET HOW MUCH WORK GOES INTO THE MAKING OF A CD, IT'S AMAZING TO THINK HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE WILLING TO SIGN UP FOR THIS LINE OF WORK!!! TRUELY A WELL CRAFTED CD IS A LABOR OF LOVE!!!) IN A WORD TO DESCRIBE WALTER AS A MAN, ARTIST AND A PRODUCER...AWESOME!!!

Scott...just got himself a new truck...it's a beauty an emerald green Ford f150 long bed, king cab, I mention this because the way Scott bought this vehicle is very much the way he appraoches his music...he does his research, he formulates a plan with a goal in mind, he goes about exicuting and ta da!!! a great result, in the case of his truck he go just what he wanted without any real hassle...paid what he wanted and got the quality he wanted!!! He works a piece of music the same way...his thought process is exacting and his results are well thought out, on more than one ocassion, I have just watched his playing with awe and envy, while I may have strengths in writing, I have always desired great musianship, and trust me I worked hard trying to achieve that goal, I only got so far...Scott on the other hand, has that great gift...and Scott being Scott he has maximised his gift...he can make a keyboard sing and the beauty of his talent is that he can sing as beautifully as he plays, I can't wait to share his gift with you!!! Ultimaley working with Scott is a clean deal, I'm still getting to know him as a man, but I can tell you this, his dad was a musician I know he is very supportve of Scotts art...Scott himself appears to be a very loving father and a very romantic man, he told me a story about his getting engaged to his wife,(who also gives him support) I gotta tell you this sorty coulda been right of a movie script...I'll just say he suprised his wife by showing up in Paris with a ring...a ring handed down from his Grandmother...it was a perfect story and he tells it well...

Earl...Earl, man this guy is a star (waiting to happen)...I mean that!!! listen to me when I tell you, his playing is inspired by angels, but it goes far beyond that, his vibe is so easy and so present, I don't really know a whole hell of alot about him, but I do know he's 25, a graduate of Brown University and based on his tremendous abilty to listen, his wit is so refreshing...say something to Earl and it could come back at you at anytime...from what I've seen he is brilliant and has a girlfriend for over a year or so, I've never met her...but this guy is a rock guy to the max...he lives and loves the music and the women who love the boys that love to rock...and this is a guy worth loving, I'm really looking forward to his guitar work on the record, to date he has only played bass...

Kelly...A member of the band Failure...played the main stage of LOLLA PALOZZA all summer and is in between records and had the time to help Walter out...this little guy is a cosmic dynamo...he's already is a star...a hardworking one...o.k. so he likes to eat and smoke...that doesn't make him a bad person does it??? Kelly has been a joy to work with, but I do think I kinda got on his nerves alittle in the studio...His drumming is so dead on, you have to love this guy...he plays with a click track like it's know bodies business!!! as rock stars go ( i mean that in a positive light)...Kelly is just that...I for one thank him for what he brought to this record, not sure if he has any overdubs left so Kelly could be done with us for now...I do know that after doing this record he was gonna take of to Vacouver B.C. for some R and R with his lady friend...lucky him I love B.C.!!!

Me...Michael Gabbay, I said to you I am a better man for doing this, I want to share a bunch of things, you know it's dawning on me, that I am sharing my goal with the world, I am willing to accept the outcome and be responcible for the results, it's just interesting reporting this info to you and sharing these feelings it's even more interesting that I have the desire to even do this...O.K. without getting to far into the money of this record I am managing that part too, but being the money man at a session sometimes gets in the way of feeling like an artist you no one of the 'boys', understand Kelly and Earl are being paid for the pressence in the studio and selected because of thier artistry...Scott enjoys that this could be a 'career' making record for him cause if you sing a hit record you not only make some good cash but you can gig for the rest of your life...and this record is full of possibilty, not to mention my driven desire to achieve my goal!!! so as an artist this guy should be on cloud 9 to be the focal point of this record, Walter is being given a token amount of cash, far below his market worth, but he is also being paid as an artist, and there I sit in the room starving to be the seen as an artist as well (I want to feel like someone is choosing me for my ability as a writer)...after all where are these songs coming from??? You have to understand know one is wrong here...I'm not at the center of thier world, they are, and but they are willing to support my dream, so I guess that will have to do, but in a perfect world I guess I wish they all believed and wanted to donate there time, (being the 'baby in my family, the last of 5...i guess that desire to feel taken care of never goes away) I feel like the songs are my children and I want everyone else to love my kids as much as I do...I don't really think thats a real possibilty but I keep on dreaming of that perfect world...reality shows up around here...all the time!!! I am starved to be acknowleged as an artist/writer, and really my pay off will come down the road in sales, cause it's one thing to write a song and record it anyone can do that...but it's a whole other deal having a hit record...well I'll just have to 'go with the flow' and make my own way!!! A bottomline to look at is I have a great team, these guys are respectively perfect and we really are well suited!!! Here's interesting point...
Michael...writer/guitar 43
Walter...producer 30 (i think)
Scott...keyboard/vocalist/father 33

We are three men who truly have the same ultimate goal (a hit record) for different reasons...to quote the 'counting crows'..."yeah we all wanna be big big stars...but we got different reasons for that!!!" The three of us as a team are mighty...we have our bases covered...writer/producer/artist...I will be changing hats soon enough and be a marketing director...I am going to learn to work the media!!! hey if madonna can do it...so can I!!!!

THATS IT!!! LETS STOP HERE...I'VE TESTED YOUR EYES...IF YOU GOT MOST OF THIS, THANKS FOR YOUR TIME...I'm not sure whats on deck next...When Walter lets me know...you'll be the next to find out...as always...stay tuned and enjoy the pics...sound bites comin son!!!

see ya,
