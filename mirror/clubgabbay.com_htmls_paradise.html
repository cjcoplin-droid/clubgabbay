Paradise Patio

A sweet smell wafts through the evening air as you sidle though the backdoor to the seaside patio, the syncopated reggae sounds drawing you into a world in which you already feel welcome. You can't help but get caught up in the beat, tap your collective toes and drink in the evening's sensory pleasures. This could be Montego Bay, and that could be the Wailers up there. The dreadlocked musicians seem to fall further into a trance as their legions sip Red Stripe beer, munch on a litte Jerk Chicken, and try to figure out just where that sweet smell is coming from. Is this the kind of place you've been looking for to make some new friends? Yah man....
