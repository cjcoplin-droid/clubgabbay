Classical Comments

As you waltz on in, the first thing you notice is the grandeur of the Steinway Grand -- a perfectly crafted instrument that is the key to this evening's seranade -- and the grace of the harp that sits to its side ready to unleash its symphony of sounds. You know they'll make beautiful music together, and you wish you could find someone with whom to do the same. The tuxedoed waiters ask which of your fellow lovers of the classics you might like to join, perhaps even chat with for a bit. You're not in an allegro, but you don't want to wait a minuet more than you have to.You look around trying to decide which movement to make your first, then come right in on the downbeat and find yourself right on 4/4 time. Hallelujah!....
