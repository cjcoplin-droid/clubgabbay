Speakeasy

The sweet melodious tones of a solitary trumpet calls your name as you rap at the door and are welcomed in. A single player stands alone on the rick-walled room's raisedstage, his cigarette burning in an ashtray, his horntaking on a life all its own/ His eyes stare blindly into nothingness as he wails away no no one.. He certainly feels it tonight, and so do you. You close your eyes and visions of Charlie Parker, John Coltrane. Lester Young, Mingus and Monk fill your mind as the as the sweet sounds siink deep into your soul. Man, you didn't know one horn could make you feel so good. You wish you had someone to share this remarkable moment with. Just then, you hear a voice. "You alone," asks the stranger? "Not anymore," you reply....
