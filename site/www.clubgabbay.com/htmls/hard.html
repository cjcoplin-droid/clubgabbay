Hard Rock Gabbay

(You're not a headbanger and punk makes you puke, but it's not quite time to hang up your rock and roll shoes just yet. The Beatles. The Stones. Bowie. Bruce. Hootie. As you enter, vintage concert footage plays on a giant-screen behind the stage to your left, and you can't help but notice the familiar insect logo on the drum set. It's been a hard day's night already and things are a little helter skelter here, and while you do have a ticket to ride you know it can't buy you love. You gotta try. You see her standing there and initially decide to let it be, but you can't do that. Yesterday you needed a little help from your friends to get back on life's long and winding road, but today you've got a feeling that she loves you and that tonight it might just come together. Dig it. The party's in progress and everyone is starting to twist and shout as the band takes the stage. You want to be her man and know you can work it out, and you hope against hope that she doesn't let you down. Perhaps you should have known better, but for the moment you decide you'd like to hold her hand. Roll over Beethoven....
