Denim & Dialogue

It's a boot scootin' Thursday and the club is starting to live up to its name and reputation. Urban cowboys fondle their Lone Stars and push back their hats as cowgirls decked out in drop-dead diamonds and dime store bangles survey the evening's offerings. There's a whole mess of folks pulled up to the vintage wall-length mohangany bar that Jesse James once sidled up to, and a couple of longtime regulars chat about the good ole days when Waylon, Willie and the boys used to stop by. You look around, two-set through the sawdust and peanut shells and psuh your tush in and out of the long rows of tables that lead to the stage. That's when you realize -- you've never seen so Tony Lama snakeskin boots in a single room in your whole dang life. Let the party begin....
