Soul Searching

Yourstage is for an evening that's been years in the making -- you never thought you would get the chance to see them again. A trio of spotlights sparkle down on stage as the crowd begins to sway to the steady rhythm of the Motown sound that vibrates through the speaker system of the darkened room. You walk in to the beat of the same old song, stop for a moment in the name of love, and shout out to some old friends. That's when you realize that new friends might be even more appealing. You join a group waiting near the deceivingly quiet dance floor. As SHE takes the stage and the music begins, these perfect strangers don't remain strangers very long....
