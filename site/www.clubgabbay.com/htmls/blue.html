The Blue Notes

It feels really good to feel so bad -- but sometimes you just don't want to be blue alone. This cavernous candle-lit room is dark, real dark, and the feeling is the same. The band is blowing out the blues like they've had it all their lives, and you pull up a seat to share the sounds, share the feelings, and think about happier times when you had it made. It's only when the music stops and the lights come on that you notice you're not alone. Your sorrow turns to smiles as you strike up a conversation, and think about turning these into even happier times than back then. Misery does, indeed, love company....
This room is intended for adults 21 and over.
