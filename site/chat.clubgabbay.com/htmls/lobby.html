Any concerns that the interior couldn't live up to expectations fade away as you enter the sprawling foyer. This place is classy and cool, from its oak-lined walls and vaulted ceiling to the overstuffed furniture and tasteful floral displays. The feeling is a satisfying mix of fashion and function, and the mood is warm, inviting and sophisticated. You're taken by the exquisite fountain that greets your entrance, its gentle waterfalls soothing your soul as you drink in the atmosphere and the excitement. Complimentary cocktails await to the left, and behind them a delicious assortment of appetizers.
