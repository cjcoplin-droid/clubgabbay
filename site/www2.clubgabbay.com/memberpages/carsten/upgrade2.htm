The Graphics Board - upgrade value = 75 %

A fast graphics board becomes more and more important in the days of multimedia.
Words like "AVI" and "MPEG" are in peoples´ mouth. There are different types of cards on the market. Most important thing on a good card is the graphicschip
(most boards use an S3-Chip) which should have at least 64 bit, latest cards use
128 bit technology. Next point is the RAM. There are different types of RAM that´s
provided on the cards. Slowest is DRAM, faster is EDO, even faster is VRAM and the
fastest currently is WRAM, provided by Matrox Inc.
Last point to look at is the so-called RAMDAC. It´s power is expressed in MHz, the faster the
better. 180 MHz or 220 MHz should be enough.
Tip: If you plan to buy a card for gaming, you should look for a card containing 2 MB EDO
or more and a 3D-Accelerator chip (e.g. Matrox). For standard office work a 2 MB DRAM card
should be enough, most cards already have AVI- and MPEG-acceleration.
For publishing, scanning, etc you should get a real high-end card with much fast RAM,
a fast RAMDAC and a 128 bit processor on it.


The CD-Drive - upgrade value = 25 %

In times of Multimedia a CD-Drive is essential. If you already have a drive and think
about upgrading to a faster one you should make a bigger step.
It isn´t worth the money to upgrade from a 4x to a 6x-speed.
Tip: Upgrade to a drive that has the double rating of your current drive.
Like harddisks there are two different types of drives again:
SCSI and IDE drives. There are the same conditions for a buy like for a harddisk.
To safe money you should think about if you need the drive much. Do you read much data
from the drive ? Or do you use it rarely ? In such case a slower drive should be enough.
A final important point on CD-drives: Watch out for error-correction !!!
The fastest drive isn´t worth anything if it can´t correct scratches on the CD it´s reading.


The Sound Board - upgrade value = 10 %

Again Multimedia was the reason for the big run on soundcards.
There aren´t big differences in soundcards, so you can buy which
one you want. The only thing you have to decide about is whether
you like to have Wavetable sound or not. Wavetable sounds
better than the sounds the soundchip on a standard card produces.
But wavetable has only got an influence on MIDI-MUSIC !!!


The Modem - upgrade value = 10 %

Internet users often see that it´s very slow online, so there might be
the thought of buying a new modem. Well, first there´s something to
think about and to figure out. You need to know what the maximum
transfer rate for you internet provider is, e.g. 28,800 baud. It´s kinda
senseless then to buy a faster modem, because you won´t be able to use
its full capacity. So you can save money there. Standard speed should
be 28,800 baud, but now there are 33,600 baud modems on the market.
Latest technology allows transfer rates up to 57,600 baud !


The Monitor - upgrade value = 20 %

If you want to buy a new monitor, think about what you´re going to do in
future. If you plan to do more in graphics you should consider buying a
bigger monitor (17"), if not, a 15" monitor should be enough.
There are two technical factors you should take a look at:
1) The vertikal frequency: Should be higher than 64 kHz.
2) The horizontal frequency: Should be higher than 100 Hz
And the most important thing is that the monitor should match the frequencies
the graphics board can produce.
