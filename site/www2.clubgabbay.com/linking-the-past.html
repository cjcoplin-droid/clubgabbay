Linking the Past

Club Gabbay lived on the early web, and traces of it are still out there -- in diaries, directories, archives, and other people's memories. This page collects the breadcrumbs: the past efforts that remember the place, so anyone wandering in can follow them back. If you find another one, tell us on the message board.

A diary from inside the rooms
Chats 1-10 -- October 15th to December 2nd 1997 -- Bloodwynd's first-person log of ten evenings chatting at Club Gabbay in the fall of 1997: the Lobby, Birds of a Feather, getting kicked out, servers dying, strangers from Australia and Missouri. He called it "a source of unthreatening social contact." The full chat list is at gitchi.net/chat.html.

The original site, as the archive kept it
Club Gabbay on the Wayback Machine -- snapshots of www.clubgabbay.com captured by the Internet Archive between 1996 and 1999. The frozen pages are where this mirror began: every room, every button, rebuilt from what the archive saved.

How the web found it back then
Old'aVista Real-Time Chat directory -- a 1996 directory listing: "Club Gabbay -- Ten different chat rooms, each with its own musical style."
Yahoo! Entertainment: Music: Real-Time Chat -- the 1994-96 Yahoo directory carried the same listing.
AndyChat changelog -- a chat client of the era added support "for the Club Gabbay web room (for a challenge)."

What we could not find
No archived copy of Club Gabbay's own guestbook has turned up anywhere -- not in the archive, not on Google, not anywhere we looked. The guestbook names we do have are the ones frozen in the 1997 Locator page. If you kept a copy of anything from the old site -- a guestbook page, a chat log, a screenshot -- the message board is the place to say so.
