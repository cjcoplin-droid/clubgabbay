The old GeoCities neighborhoods
Five neighborhoods, the way they were -- fan-made portals, each with its own board

GeoCities sorted its free home pages into themed neighborhoods, each with its own directory, chat rooms, bulletin boards and volunteer Community Leaders. Yahoo shut it down in October 2009, but the neighborhoods live on in the archives -- and now here, as fan-made portals. Pick one, browse the old pages, and leave a message on its board if you were there.

  • Hollywood -- Films and TV -- the very first GeoCities neighborhood, July 1995 (board)
  • SiliconValley -- Computers, hardware, software and programming -- August 1995 (board)
  • SunsetStrip -- Rock, grunge, punk, blues and the club scene -- July 1995 (board)
  • Area51 -- Science fiction and fantasy -- April 1996 (board)
  • WestHollywood -- Gay, lesbian, bisexual and transgender topics -- July 1995 (board)
