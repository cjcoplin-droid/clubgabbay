Hollywood
Films and TV -- the very first GeoCities neighborhood, July 1995

Hollywood was the very first GeoCities neighborhood, opened July 1995 -- films and TV, actors and actresses. If you loved movies, this was your block. Its suburbs had names like Hills, Star, Studio and Cinema, and every homesteader picked a four-digit block to call home.

This is a fan-made portal, not the original GeoCities page. The original is gone; the homesteads live on in the archives linked below. Were you a homesteader here? Enter the Hollywood chat or leave a message on the Hollywood board -- say what your address was, who you remember, and where life took you.

The neighborhood chats are for adults 21 and over.
The suburbs of Hollywood

When a neighborhood filled up, the volunteer Community Leaders opened suburbs -- each with its own blocks. Hollywood's suburbs were: Academy, Agency, Backdrop, Boulevard, Bungalow, Camera, Chateau, Cinema, Club, Derby, Diva, Film, Guild, Heights, Highrise, Hills, Interview, Land, Location, Lot, Makeup, Mansion, Movie, Palace, Park, Pavillion, Picture, Prop, Screen, Set, Star, Studio, Theater, Trailer, Video.
