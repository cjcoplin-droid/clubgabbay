SunsetStrip
Rock, grunge, punk, blues and the club scene -- July 1995

SunsetStrip opened July 1995 for music -- rock, grunge, punk, blues and the club scene. Its suburbs read like a night out: Vine, Alley, Palms, Studio, Towers, Backstage, Frontrow.

This is a fan-made portal, not the original GeoCities page. The original is gone; the homesteads live on in the archives linked below. Were you a homesteader here? Enter the SunsetStrip chat or leave a message on the SunsetStrip board -- say what your address was, who you remember, and where life took you.

The suburbs of SunsetStrip

When a neighborhood filled up, the volunteer Community Leaders opened suburbs -- each with its own blocks. SunsetStrip's suburbs were: Alley, Amphitheater, Arena, Auditorium, Backstage, Balcony, Bar, Basement, Bass, Beat, Birdland, Bistro, Booth, Cabaret, Club, Concert, Derby, Diner, Disco, Event, Exhibit, Festival, Floor, Frontrow, Gala, Garage, Hall, Hotel, Limo, Lobby, Loft, Loge, Lounge, Mezzanine, Palace, Palladium, Palms, Pavilion, Performance, Pit, Plaza, Scene, Set, Show, Stadium, Stage, Street, Studio, Tempo, Theater, Towers, Underground, Venue, Villa, Vine.
