Area51
Science fiction and fantasy -- April 1996

Area51 opened April 1996 for science fiction and fantasy -- and conspiracy theories. Its suburbs were pure imagination: Vault, Stargate, Starship, Nebula, Dimension, Dreamworld.

This is a fan-made portal, not the original GeoCities page. The original is gone; the homesteads live on in the archives linked below. Were you a homesteader here? Enter the Area51 chat or leave a message on the Area51 board -- say what your address was, who you remember, and where life took you.

The suburbs of Area51

When a neighborhood filled up, the volunteer Community Leaders opened suburbs -- each with its own blocks. Area51's suburbs were: Alien, Atlantis, Aurora, Capsule, Cavern, Chamber, Comet, Corridor, Crater, Dimension, Dreamworld, Dunes, Dungeon, Hole, Hollow, Jupiter, Keep, Labyrinth, Lair, Matrix, Meteor, Nebula, Neptune, Nova, Omega, Orion, Planet, Portal, Quadrant, Quark, Quasar, Rampart, Realm, Saturn, Shadowlands, Shire, Shuttle, Stargate, Starship, Station, Underworld, Vault, Zone.
