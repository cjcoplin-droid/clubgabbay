SiliconValley
Computers, hardware, software and programming -- August 1995

SiliconValley opened August 1995 for computers, hardware, software and programming -- the techies' neighborhood. Its suburbs were named for the trade: Heights, Park, Pines, Chip, Code, Modem, Server.

This is a fan-made portal, not the original GeoCities page. The original is gone; the homesteads live on in the archives linked below. Were you a homesteader here? Leave a message on the SiliconValley board -- say what your address was, who you remember, and where life took you.

The suburbs of SiliconValley

When a neighborhood filled up, the volunteer Community Leaders opened suburbs -- each with its own blocks. SiliconValley's suburbs were: Bay, Bit, Board, Bridge, Byte, Cable, Campus, Chip, Circuit, Code, Disk, Drive, File, Foothills, Garage, Grid, Hardware, Haven, Heights, Hills, Horizon, Hub, Lab, Lakes, Modem, Monitor, Mouse, Network, Node, Office, Orchard, Park, Peaks, Pines, Platform, Port, Program, Ridge, Screen, Sector, Server, Software, Station, Storage, Vista, Way.
