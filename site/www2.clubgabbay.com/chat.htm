The rules, plainly:

- You must be 21 or older to enter the chat rooms.
- Your messages are your own — you are responsible for what you say.
- No harassment, threats, or illegal content. No sexual content involving minors, ever.
- Do not share anyone's private information without their consent.
- No impersonation, scams, or fraud.
- Public messages pass through an automated word filter. There is no live human moderator.
- Whispers are private and unmonitored.
- If someone breaks the rules, report it — reports get looked at, and breaking the rules can get you removed.
- The full terms are in the fine print.
