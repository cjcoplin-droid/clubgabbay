# Where to drop the Club Gabbay URL — research, 2026-11-09

Research only. **Nothing was posted.** Every URL below was curl-checked live
(status shown) on 2026-11-09. The site to share is
https://clubgabbay.onrender.com — a fan-run revival of the 1996-1999
GeoCities chat rooms (live chat rooms, GeoCities neighborhood portals,
AOL Chat, retro messenger rooms, message board, Linking the Past page).

The 90s way: sign a guestbook with a genuine message and your URL, or
submit the site to a webring/directory that asks for submissions. Never
spray the link. One-shot places (noted) get exactly one attempt.

## Ranked list — best fit first

### 1. Ytoo! — guestbook + suggest-a-site (BEST FIT)
- URL: https://ytoo.org/gbook.php (guestbook) and https://ytoo.org/suggest.php (suggest a site)
- Status: both 200.
- What it is: a Yahoo!-style old-web portal. Its guestbook is a live,
  signable form (name + message, "Sign Guestbook") and it has an explicit
  "Suggest a site" page with guidelines for sharing a link.
- Fit: the single best match — a guestbook that *asks* for site
  suggestions, in the exact Yahoo idiom Club Gabbay revives. Sign the
  guestbook with a real message + the URL, and suggest the site too.

### 2. TheOldNet Webring — submit the site
- URL: https://webring.theoldnet.com/submit
- Status: 200; working submit form (email, site name, site URL, banner
  URL, description, "is retro" flag).
- What it is: the webring for The Old Net — a project that brings back
  browsing the 90s web. It explicitly invites 90s-themed pages, and a
  site does NOT have to be old-browser compatible to join.
- Fit: a permanent webring listing in front of exactly the right
  audience. Submit once; add the webring widget to the Club Gabbay
  landing page after acceptance (the ring asks members to carry it).

### 3. Retronaut Webring — join
- URL: https://webring.dinhe.net/ (use the "Join the webring" link on the page)
- Status: 200; a "Join the webring" link is on the homepage.
- What it is: a webring "for fans of the oldweb who miss the feeling of
  the pre social media era internet."
- Fit: on-topic and open. Club Gabbay is exactly the feeling it is for.

### 4. MelonLand Forum + Surf Club Webring — join the community
- URL: https://forum.melonland.net/
- Status: 200.
- What it is: a creative hub/forum for digital art, making websites,
  net art and Neocities — the old-web revival community. Members get
  their site listed in the Surf Club Webring (a community site
  directory) and can post about their site on the forum.
- Fit: good, but it is a *community join*, not a quick drop. Accounts
  are manually approved (can take up to a week) and the signup asks for
  an intro + links to your site/art. Caveats: the forum is closed on
  Mondays; and it does not accept AI bots or AI-generated posts, and
  prefers members not register with "totally AI generated sites" —
  Club Gabbay is a hand-directed mirror of a real 1996-1999 archive,
  but say so plainly in the intro if asked. Best approached as joining
  the community, then sharing the site once inside.

### 5. Wiby — submit the page (search engine)
- URL: https://wiby.me/submit/
- Status: 200.
- What it is: a search engine for simple, mostly text-based, old-web
  style pages. Submission-based; it also has a "surprise me" random
  page feature.
- Fit: good for discoverability. Submit the landing page. Note: Wiby
  indexes pages with little-to-no JavaScript, so the mostly-static
  landing page is the right page to submit, not the JS chat rooms.

### 6. ooh.directory — suggest a site
- URL: https://ooh.directory/ (use the "Suggest" link)
- Status: 200; suggest page 200.
- What it is: "a calm directory of personal websites that rewards slow
  browsing."
- Fit: a quiet, human-curated directory of personal sites. Suggest the
  site; it fits the personal/handmade ethos.

### 7. Curlie — suggest a site (human-edited directory)
- URL: https://curlie.org/docs/en/add.html (instructions; the suggest
  form lives on the category page you pick)
- Status: 200.
- What it is: the modern successor to the DMOZ open directory, kept by
  volunteer editors. You pick the single most relevant category and
  suggest the site once, with a short objective description (<25 words).
- Fit: a real directory listing, but slow — editors review by hand and
  it can take a week to months. Worth doing once; do not re-submit.

### 8. r/InternetIsBeautiful — one shot, high reach
- URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/InternetIsBeautiful/
- Status: live (Reddit blocked curl verification, 403; rules verified
  from two independent rule-readings dated July-Oct 2026).
- What it is: 16.6M-member subreddit for free, interactive,
  single-purpose websites. A free site with no signup is the sanctioned
  content type.
- Fit: the biggest single audience, but strict and one-shot: the domain
  can be submitted ONCE ever; the account must follow the 90/10 rule
  (90% of recent activity not promoting your own site); no sites whose
  primary content is AI-produced; no sites asking for personal info.
  Club Gabbay is free and needs no signup, which fits — but only post
  from an established account with real non-promo history, and only
  once. Read the live sidebar rules before posting; they change.

### 9. r/GeoCities — on-topic, read the rules first
- URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/GeoCities/
- Status: live (Reddit blocked curl verification, 403; subreddit rules
  could not be verified from here).
- What it is: the subreddit for GeoCities itself. A post about a
  GeoCities chat-room revival is on-topic by subject.
- Fit: the most on-topic subreddit, but its self-promotion rules are
  unverified — read the sidebar and any pinned threads before posting,
  and disclose that it is your own revival. Do not assume a link post
  is welcome.

### 10. r/SideProject — allowed, low nostalgia fit
- URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/
- Status: live (rules page verified empty in multiple 2026 snapshots —
  effectively unrestricted for side projects).
- What it is: a subreddit for sharing side projects.
- Fit: allowed and low-risk, but the audience is builders/startups,
  not 90s-nostalgia people. Fine as a "I rebuilt a 1996 chat site"
  show-and-tell; expect builder interest, not old Gabbay members.

- **Endless Orbit Webring — CLOSED.** Listed as closed in two webring
  indexes (2026). Do not use.
- **Hotline Webring — no join path found.** https://hotlinewebring.club/
  (200) is a live member list, but no submit/join link could be found,
  and indexes disagree on whether it is open or closed. Not a solid drop.
- **Space Tavern Webring — live but no join path found.**
  https://darkspider5.neocities.org/ (200). Listed as active in a 2025
  webring list, but no join/submit mechanism was found on the page.
- **404PageFound — criteria exclude revivals.**
  https://www.404pagefound.com/criteria/ — it showcases genuine
  1994-2001 sites last modified pre-2000, and explicitly says
  post-2004 sites "meant to look dated" do not qualify. Club Gabbay
  is a 2026 revival, so it does not fit. Do not submit.
- **Discovery Webring — Neocities-only.** Requires a *.neocities.org
  URL; Club Gabbay is on Render, so it cannot join.
- **r/nostalgia — RULE-FATAL.** Its rules ban "posts to webstores,
  blogs or websites." Do not post the link there.
- **Pixel Moondust guestbook — could not verify.** The GeoCities
  backgrounds site (pixelmoondust.neocities.org, 200) invites visitors
  to "sign my guestbook with your site," but no signable guestbook URL
  could be found on its pages. Not listable without the link.
- **Old'aVista — not a submission target.** https://oldavista.com/
  searches archived (Wayback) sites; it is not a directory for a new
  revival. Do not submit.
- **Neocities directory — not applicable.** Club Gabbay is not hosted
  on Neocities, so it cannot be listed there.

## How to use this (the 90s way)

1. Start with #1 (Ytoo guestbook + suggest) and #2 (TheOldNet webring)
   — both are built for exactly this, and both are permanent.
2. Guestbooks: write a real message as a person who was there ("I hung
   out in the Lobby in '97, rebuilt it, come say hi") and leave the URL.
   One genuine signature beats ten bare links.
3. Webrings/directories: submit once each, then add any required widget
   or badge to the Club Gabbay landing page after acceptance.
4. Reddit: #8 is one shot ever for the domain, from an established
   account; #9 needs a sidebar read first; #10 is the safe backup.
5. Do not re-submit to the same place, and do not drop the link in
   places that did not ask for it — that is what reads as spam.
